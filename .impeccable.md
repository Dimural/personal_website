## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and fellow engineers landing on Dimural Murat's
personal portfolio — usually on desktop, sometimes mobile, often skimming for
"is this person good?" in under a minute. The job to be done: convey craft and
taste quickly, then make it trivial to reach out or explore work.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **engineered, tactile, considered.** Quietly confident, not loud.
The site itself is the proof of work — every detail is intentional. Should evoke
calm focus and a sense of "this person sweats the details," not hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm dark luxury (restyled 2026-07-12; replaced the earlier green/techy scheme).
Deep espresso-charcoal canvas with a single ambient Aurora glow — warm
bronze/amber embers — behind the hero name. Generous negative space, restrained
motion, one muted champagne-gold accent used sparingly. Should feel like a
high-end hotel bar: classy, sleek, calm. Anti-references: terminal/hacker
green-on-black, neon accents, mono uppercase "techy" labels, cluttered WebGL,
glassmorphism everywhere, centered everything.

### Type System
- **Display:** Clash Display (Fontshare) — for the name and section titles.
- **Body / UI / Labels:** Geist — prose, nav, buttons; small uppercase labels
  are letterspaced Geist 500 (never monospace — Geist Mono was removed as part
  of the de-tech restyle).
- **Accent serif:** Gambetta italic 500 (Fontshare) — ONLY for italic accent
  words inside section titles (e.g. "*feel*"), always in the gold accent.
  (Instrument Serif deliberately avoided.)

### Palette (OKLCH, neutrals tinted warm amber ~60–86h)
- `--bg`        oklch(0.165 0.010 65)   espresso near-black
- `--surface`   oklch(0.205 0.012 65)   lifted panel
- `--text`      oklch(0.958 0.010 85)   warm cream
- `--text-dim`  oklch(0.726 0.020 78)   secondary
- `--text-faint`oklch(0.548 0.018 74)   tertiary / labels
- `--border`    oklch(0.305 0.018 70)
- `--accent`    oklch(0.815 0.095 86)   muted champagne gold (rare, 10%)
- Aurora stops: warm embers `#6f5230 → #8a6a3e → #7a5158` (bronze → amber → rosewood)

### Design Principles
1. **One signature, everything else quiet.** Aurora is the only ambient effect;
   CardSwap is the one interactive focal moment. Nothing else competes.
2. **Space is the design.** Lean on rhythm and negative space over chrome.
3. **Gold is rare.** The accent earns its power by appearing seldom.
4. **Left-aligned, asymmetric.** Avoid centering everything; let layouts breathe.
5. **Restraint in motion.** Smooth ease-out reveals, no bounce/elastic.
