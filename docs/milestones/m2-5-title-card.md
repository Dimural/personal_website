# M2.5 — Title card

**Status:** in progress

Design: [title-card landing](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-title-card-landing-design.md).
Plan: [plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-title-card-landing.md).

## Goal

The landing reads as a calm personal website over the living sleeping scene. Space reveals that the
scenery is a world you can walk in; the log is where you go back to sleep and the card returns.

## Scope

1. Title card (name, bio, experience with icon slots, projects), "press space" button; space-only wake;
   no name tag, no small heading.
2. Walk back to the log, press space to sleep; the card fades back in.
3. Birds over the meadow; rabbits that hop and flee the walker.

## Done when

- [x] Card shows over the sleeping scene; only space (or the button) wakes; card fades out; phone layout stacks.
- [x] Space at the log (after walking away) puts the figure back to sleep and brings the card back; mashing space is safe.
- [ ] Birds and rabbits move calmly; rabbits flee; still 60fps.
- [ ] `npm run build` passes.
